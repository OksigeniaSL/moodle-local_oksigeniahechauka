On the television, open the address of the screens: it shows a code and a QR.
Scan the QR with your phone, or open the address on your phone and type the code.
Say whether it is a new screen or whether it replaces one you had.
Give it a name that says where it is and, if you like, its groups.
Press «Start»: in a few seconds the screen shows what is its.
In «Screens and groups» you see it with its state, and its sheet sets its hours.
