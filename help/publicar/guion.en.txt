To publish something on the screens, press «Add».
At the top you choose what it is: a notice, a photo or a video, an event, a ticker line…
Write the notice. Short reads better from afar.
On the right you see how it will look on the screen as you type.
Choose when it comes out and on which screens: all of them, a group or just one.
Press «Publish on…» and within a minute it is on the screens.
