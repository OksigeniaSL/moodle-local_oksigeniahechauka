# Changelog

## 0.21.0

- **Two new Surprises, each moving in a way none of the others does**, from the art direction: the frog leaps in from
  under the bottom edge, lands right on the floor and crosses in hops, then sticks out its tongue or puffs up its throat;
  the robin flies in, lands on the edge of the ticker and hops along it, then fluffs up or sings and flies off. On a floor
  too high for the leap to read, the frog hops in from the side. They join a centre that had chosen every one of the others;
  one that left someone out keeps its choice.

## 0.20.0

- **Every menu of the day on one slide**, where the centre serves several: the menus in columns, each headed by its letter
  (or the word that names it) large, as people look for it from afar, and the dishes in rows; a dish they all share
  runs across, the allergens beside each dish, and pages by distance (at 8 m, one menu at a time). From the art direction.
- **The canteen's hours**, in «Your centre» beside the end of the day, when the site has Oksigenia Meals (11:00 to 14:00
  by default): the slide of every menu comes often then and now and then the rest of the day; with several menus,
  «Today» does not take the first as its headline then. They replace the fixed midday of the player.

## 0.19.2

- **Birthdays with their photos also right after a screen starts.** When the birthday slide or band came before the
  photos were on the device (a screen just started or updated, with many files to bring), the player asked for each photo
  by its address, which needs the screen's key that a picture does not carry: the server refused it and a creature took
  its place. Now a photo is shown only from the device; the big slide waits for its photos, ten minutes at most, and the
  band puts each photo in as soon as it arrives.

## 0.19.1

- **A destination the link shortener refuses for good is not asked for again for a month** (an address outside the
  domains of its key, or none): before, it was asked every hour, spending the limit every plugin of the site shares.
  The shared client of the shortener says now why it gave nothing, and a «too many at once» waits as long as it asks.

## 0.19.0

- **«The library celebrates»**, a new source where the site has Oksigenia Ayvurenda: what the library celebrates this week,
  in its own words and never with a person's name (the books lent this year, a book reaching its hundredth loan, the
  busiest day, readers at the goal of a challenge, a class that met its own), four at most. A slide of its own, one in
  sixteen: the library's rosette in the colours of the skin, pinned to the cover when the moment is about a book. On by
  default, off from «Start» or the settings.
- **Upright photos keep their shape** beside «Today» at every distance, not only with the birthday band; at 8 m on a
  landscape screen two of them no longer share a slide, where their captions were too small to read: each comes in its turn.
- **«Screens and groups»: no envelope beside one's own name.**
- **The guide made again**, with every picture taken anew and its PDFs in Spanish and English.

## 0.18.0

- **«Zones», one more composition in the turn**: a column with its own carousel (upright photos, the weather, the coming
  days) beside the wide part, which takes a landscape photo or video or the card of the screen's place. It comes now and
  then on informative and mixed screens, never stays, and never leaves an empty box: what has nothing to show gives its room
  to the rest.
- **On the day someone has a birthday, a band congratulates them all day long**, in every composition of informative and
  mixed screens: a garland in the four colours, confetti on the paper and one face at a time, with a small bounce when it
  changes and then still. Never on a showcase screen, nor during the big celebration of its turn.
- **The place of each screen**, in its sheet: a library, a canteen, an office, with its hours by days and the days it
  closes. The screen says beside the clock whether it is open and until when («Open · until 17:00», «Closed · opens on
  Monday at 8:00»), and the zones show the week's hours. In Spanish, the centre chooses «Abierta» or «Abierto».
- **The centre's sentence**, in «Your centre»: its motto, said whole in the ticker every few sentences with the centre's
  logo, never typed letter by letter; from 8 m, a headline of its own. Up to 90 characters, what fits on any horizontal
  screen read from 5 m.
- Three upright photos keep the gap of the rest, and a caption with a word wider than its photo goes instead of being cut.
- The important notice no longer disappears at 3 and 5 m when the ticker has no sentences.

## 0.17.3

## 0.15.0

## 0.14.0

- **The QR codes go through the site's link shortener.** Every web a screen shows as a QR (a picture's or a video's, an album's, a book's, where to start, the page that names a new screen) becomes its short address when the site has a shortener compatible with YOURLS, written whole under the code. The server asks for it in the background, so a screen never waits; until then, the long one. When the shortener draws its own QR codes (as nur.click does, in the centre's colours and with its logo), the screens show that one, kept like any picture so it shows without network; otherwise, the code the player draws.
- **A new drawing for the QR codes the player draws itself**: round dots with square corners, in black, the one that read as well as the plain code with every decoder in the art direction's bench; on the page that names a new screen, Hechauka's mark in the middle when the address is short. The same look is asked of nur.click. The code at rest is bigger (200 px at 1080p), and an address with «ñ» or «€» is encoded right.
- A screen waiting to be named keeps its code while it waits, up to half a day, instead of a new one every quarter of an hour: whoever is typing it is not cut short, and the shortener gets one link, not dozens.

## 0.13.0

How much moves comes from the skin (sober, elegant, playful), and a screen that keeps still gets the same order with fades only. Only transform and opacity, for Chromium 56 and the Raspberry Pi.
- The countdown at 8 m is a board of flaps as tall as the block, with the words beside it, so the number is read first; what falls due today has a band of the accent.
- A lead's headline never leaves a short word alone in a line.
- The urgent notice grows with the distance it is read from, steps down before a long one overflows, and its clock is never under the least size.

## 0.10.2

- The card of a Raspberry Pi screen, built again with what 0.10.1 fixed: a new screen with no hours keeps itself up to date from its first day.

## 0.10.1

- **A screen that is never off keeps itself up to date.** Its device kept what it had downloaded in memory, and the reboot of each night lost it before the day it waited for had gone by, so a screen with no hours could go on for ever without taking a new version. What is ready now stays on the card through a reboot, and goes in during the small hours of the night, before that reboot.
- The time a screen has been on no longer jumps when a device with no battery gets the time from the network, and a device that has just started no longer reloads the page that same night.

## 0.10.0

- **One card for every centre.** A centre that has only installed the plugin prepares its Raspberry Pi screens by itself: the panel page «A Raspberry Pi for the screen» says what it takes, links the card's image (published with the plugin's releases) and Raspberry Pi Imager, and gives the file that tells the card which Moodle it belongs to, with the screen's name on the network, its time zone and, if wanted, a maintenance key. «Add a screen» and «Get started» link to it.
- **A screen with no Moodle asks for it**: on the television, typed with a keyboard, or from a phone on the same network through the QR it shows. It checks that a Moodle with Hechauka answers there and says clearly what went wrong if not (no network, a name that does not exist, a website without Hechauka…), and keeps trying by itself. `sudo hechauka-direccion` moves a screen to another Moodle by SSH.
- The first boot of a card waits for the clock before updating the system: with the wrong time, the signatures of the repositories were taken as not valid yet and the installation went on with old package lists.
- Each screen takes a name of its own on the network (from the panel's file, or from its serial number), so two screens of one centre never share one.
- In the panel, what changes under the pointer only does so on devices with a pointer: a tap on a tablet no longer leaves it stuck.

## 0.7.0 (2026-11-01)

The screens, read at a glance: the art direction's design of the compositions on the player.

- **One thing big in each slide**, like the front page of a newspaper: what leads (the menu, a notice, the important
  notice, an event or the countdown, a different one each time; around midday the menu first) and the rest in a third,
  which turns a page with each slide. The important notice stays fixed on top, in the accent's colour, with its mark.
- **The letters by the distance**: each screen says in its sheet from where it is read (3, 5 or 8 m) and whether it is
  behind glass, and every size comes from that. From 5 m no week; from 8 m no thirds and no ticker: «Today» comes one
  thing at a time, big, and the ticker's sentences come as slides of their own (`titular`, also for a countdown).
- **What each screen is for**, in its sheet: informative, showcase (only pictures and videos over the whole screen, their
  headline big, and their web with a QR, for people from outside and from afar) or mixed (also the weather in a third:
  today big and the next three days). And another use at some hours: an entrance informative on weekdays and a showcase
  at the weekend. New fields `use` and `useplan` in the manifest, `distance` and `glass` in its `screen`.
- **A web for a picture or a video**: on a showcase it shows with a big QR to open it on a phone, and the address
  written short. The QR is made in the player (qrcode-generator, MIT), with no network.
- **Landscape videos have their own place** (`apaisado`): two thirds with their band under them, playing at once, and the
  rest in the other third.
- **The ticker, one sentence at a time**, instead of running; a long one is cut at a comma into two.
- **Headlines by steps and in even lines**, measured in the player (Chromium 56 has no `text-wrap: balance`).
- **«Tomorrow» at the end of the day:** each centre sets in «Tu centro» when its day ends (16:00 by default). From then
  until midnight «Today» shows tomorrow's events under «Tomorrow», the coming days start after it, and the menu switches
  at the same hour. New field `dayend` in the manifest.
- «Today» lists the events that take place today by their own dates: an event shown days before its day stays in the
  coming days, and one of several days is on each of them.
- The week has a strip with what comes next and matters.
- The events come a month ahead and the weather the next three days, for what comes next: the agenda that scrolls.

## 0.3.0 (2026-10-31)

- Each screen has its own page: how it is doing (on, off by its hours, or how long without a signal), the version of
  its player, its size, and a warning when its clock or time zone disagrees with the site's, since it runs its
  schedule by its own clock. There, its name, layout, light or dark, groups and hours on.
- A warning when a screen stops answering: after 15 minutes without a heartbeat within its hours, the people who run
  the screens (or the site admins, if nobody has the permission) get a Moodle notification with a link to the screen,
  and another one when it comes back. A screen that has just reached its hours has time to start first. It can be
  turned off for each screen.
- Videos made ready for the screens: with `ffmpeg` set in the settings, an ad hoc task converts each uploaded video,
  one at a time and with two threads, to what every screen plays (H.264 in MP4, 1080p and 30 fps at most, upright,
  without sound), with a still and a blurred still. Until it is ready the screens do not get it; if it fails, or after
  an hour, they get it as it was uploaded. Without `ffmpeg`, the panel says so.
- Fixed: the settings and the forms asked Moodle for strings it does not have («logo», «days»), which broke the
  installation of the tests. A test now checks every string the plugin asks for.
